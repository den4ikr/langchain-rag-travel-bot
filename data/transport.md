# Budapest Public Transportation Guide

## Overview

Budapest has an extensive public transportation network operated by BKK. The network includes metro lines, trams, buses, trolleybuses, and suburban railway services.

Public transport is useful for reaching major attractions, travelling between Buda and Pest, and exploring areas outside the central tourist district.

## Metro

Budapest has four metro lines:

- **M1:** The historic Millennium Underground, serving central Pest and the City Park area.
- **M2:** Connects parts of Buda with central Pest and the eastern side of the city.
- **M3:** Runs through important areas of Pest.
- **M4:** Connects Kelenföld in Buda with the Keleti railway station area.

Metro services are useful for covering longer distances quickly. Routes and service conditions should be checked before travelling.

## Trams

Trams are convenient for both everyday transport and sightseeing.

Tram 2 runs along the Pest side of the Danube and offers views of the river and major landmarks.

Trams 4 and 6 serve important central corridors and operate across the Grand Boulevard area.

Other tram routes connect residential districts, transport hubs, and attractions.

## Buses

Buses cover areas not directly served by the metro and trams. Some routes connect the city centre with the Buda hills and other outer districts.

Airport transport options can change, so visitors should check the current BKK journey planner and official airport transport information before departure.

## Tickets and Passes

Budapest offers different ticket and travel-pass options. The best choice depends on the length of the stay, the number of journeys, and eligibility for discounts.

Before travelling:

- Check current ticket prices and validity conditions.
- Confirm whether a ticket covers the intended transport service.
- Validate or activate tickets according to the applicable rules.
- Keep your ticket or pass available for inspection.
- Check whether special services require a separate ticket.

Do not assume that every ticket or pass is valid on every transport service.

## Planning a Journey

For a reliable journey plan:

1. Identify your starting point and destination.
2. Check the official BKK journey planner.
3. Compare walking, metro, tram, and bus options.
4. Review transfer requirements.
5. Check for service disruptions or temporary changes.

Journey times vary depending on traffic, waiting times, and service conditions.

## Accessibility

Accessibility varies by station, vehicle, and route. Travellers who need step-free access should verify the accessibility of their complete journey before departure.

## Practical Travel Tips

- Check the latest service information before travelling.
- Allow extra time during busy periods.
- Keep belongings secure in crowded vehicles.
- Plan your return journey before visiting attractions outside the centre.
- Use official BKK information for current schedules, fares, and travel rules.
